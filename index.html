BEFORE YOUR FIRST SESSION

Your kit.
Your computer.
A clear starting point.

Designed around an electronic drum kit connected by USB MIDI. A microphone is optional for kick detection and local recordings.

  1. 01

    Connect and map your kit

    Select your MIDI input and check that each pad appears as the right drum voice. Use headphones for the click.

  2. 02

    Align your timing

    Run calibration for your input and output setup. Repeat it when changing devices. Compensation includes your playing bias and is not a laboratory latency measurement.

  3. 03

    Start slow and save a backup

    Choose a manageable tempo. Review a few runs before increasing it. Export your history regularly, especially before updates or changing computers.

A few things to know.

Is this a seven-day trial?

No. This beta is free and has no time limit or payment requirement. Paid access may be introduced in a future version. Any future pricing and trial terms will be announced separately.

Where does my practice data go?

Sessions, settings, and optional recordings are stored locally. The practice coach runs on your computer without an AI account, API key, or uploading your history. Export backups yourself; the app does not provide cloud sync.

This project page has no analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub hosts the page and downloads and may process standard connection information. Feedback submitted on GitHub is public: remove personal information from any attachments.

Can it tell me whether my Moeller motion is correct?

It can identify patterns in hit spacing and MIDI dynamics. It cannot see your grip, rebound, tension, or movement. Use the feedback to choose what to practice and review physical technique with a teacher or video.

What should beta testers expect?

Timing depends on your devices, calibration, and system performance. Hardware compatibility still needs broader testing. Mac and Windows have some differences, including audio routing and recording access. Export your history before updates and check the release notes for current limits.

Will a normal acoustic kit work?

MIDI provides the pad-by-pad evidence used for drum grading and dynamics. The optional microphone path detects kick impacts; this beta does not transcribe a full acoustic kit from one microphone.
